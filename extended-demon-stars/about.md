# Extended Demon Stars

Extended Demon Stars gives Demon-rated Geometry Dash levels a much larger displayed star value.

## Rules

- Non-Demons are untouched.
- Normal Insane levels remain their normal 8–9 stars.
- The Nightmare, the easiest Easy Demon, displays **100**.
- Easy Demon: 100–199
- Medium Demon: 200–399
- Hard Demon: 400–699
- Insane Demon: 700–999
- Extreme Demon: 1000+

The displayed number is cosmetic. Completing a Demon still gives Geometry Dash's normal reward.

## Difficulty weighting

From highest to lowest influence:

1. Length
2. Frame-perfects
3. Timing
4. Precision
5. Visibility
6. Awkwardness
7. Game mode

Extreme Demons additionally use AREDL placement. The top of the list uses an exponential curve so the difference between very high placements becomes much larger.

AREDL data is downloaded asynchronously and cached locally.
